What is V-Fusion?

V-Fusion is my resource pack for Minecraft Java Edition. It adds animations and small tweaks while keeping the familiar vanilla look and feel.

Installation

  1. Download the pack as a .zip (button on this page). Don't unzip it.
  2. In Minecraft open Options → Resource Packs → Open Pack Folder.
  3. Move the .zip into that folder.
  4. Back in Minecraft, select V-Fusion and click Done.
